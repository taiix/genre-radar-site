What's in the email

  1. New releases in your tags. Everything from the last 7 days, sorted by how many reviews it got.
  2. Release volume. Was this a busy week or a quiet one, compared to the last month.
  3. Launch prices. The median price of recent releases, so you know what the shelf looks like.
  4. Your launch window. paid Top-wishlisted games in your tags announced within a week of your date, biggest first.
  5. Wishlist rankings. paid The most-wishlisted upcoming games in your tags, and who's climbing.
  6. Fastest-growing games. paid Who gained the most reviews this week, with a rough sales range.
  7. A short summary. paid Two or three sentences on what changed and whether it matters for you.
why this exists

Built by a dev who kept checking Steam by hand

Picking a release date is mostly guessing. You look at the calendar, scroll the upcoming list, and hope nobody big lands on the same week. I wanted the boring part automated and sent to me.

All numbers come from public Steam store pages. Sales ranges are rough (reviews × 30 to 60) and the email says so. No dashboards to log into.

Questions

Which tags can I track?

Any of Steam's 429 user tags, spelled the way Steam spells them. The signup form suggests them as you type.

When does the email arrive?

Mondays at 07:00 UTC. The data is refreshed every night, so it covers the full week before.

How accurate are the sales numbers?

Not very, and they're labelled that way. They use the common "reviews × 30–60" rule of thumb. Treat them as a size class, not a figure.

How do I change my tags or cancel?

Cancel or change your plan in the account page. To change tags, reply to any email or write to contact@stanmakes.games.

Who handles payments?

Polar runs the checkout and invoices, and takes care of VAT. I never see your card.
